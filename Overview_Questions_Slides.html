ហេតុអ្វីត្រូវប្រើ Scaling Factor √dₖ? តើវាការពារ Vanishing Gradient យ៉ាងដូចម្តេច?
Why use √dₖ, and how does it prevent vanishing gradients?
Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax( QKᵀ / √dₖ ) V

Answer

  • If Q and K have mean 0 and variance 1, then Var(q·k) = dₖ. With dₖ = 64, the scores have a standard deviation of about 8.
  • Large scores push softmax into saturation (almost one-hot), where its gradient p(1−p) ≈ 0, so the model stops learning.
  • Dividing by √dₖ brings the variance back to 1. Softmax stays in its sensitive range and backpropagation stays stable.

Real-world analogy

A microphone turned up too loud clips every sound at maximum, so you lose all detail. √dₖ is the volume knob that brings the signal back to a level you can hear.

២
បើគ្មាន Positional Encoding តើ Transformer នឹងប្រឈមមុខនឹងបញ្ហាអ្វីខ្លះ?
Without positional encoding, what problems would a Transformer face?

Answer: the model becomes a "bag of words"

  • Self-attention is permutation-invariant: shuffle the words and you get the same set of outputs.
  • Can't tell who did what to whom, so subject and object get confused.
  • Negation scope, questions vs statements, and time order all break down.
  • Translation and generation produce the wrong word order.
  • Worse for Khmer: Khmer has no conjugation or case endings, so grammatical roles depend almost entirely on word order (S–V–O).
Fix: PE(pos,2i) = sin(pos/100002i/d),  PE(pos,2i+1) = cos(…)

Khmer example: same words, opposite meaning

ស្ដេចទៅបាញ់សត្វ

"The king goes to shoot the animal."

សត្វទៅបាញ់ស្ដេច

"The animal goes to shoot the king."

Without PE → both sentences give identical representations.

Real-world analogy

Concert seat numbers: the same people in different seats form a different arrangement. PE gives every word its "seat number".

តើ Attention Weights អាចយកមកធ្វើជាភស្តុតាងនៃការរៀបចំវាក្យសម្ព័ន្ធខ្មែរបានដែរឬទេ?
Can attention weights serve as evidence of Khmer syntactic structure?
VERDICT: PARTIALLY. SUPPORTING EVIDENCE, NOT PROOF

✔ What supports it

  • Some BERT/XLM-R heads line up with subject → verb and adjective → noun links (Clark et al., 2019).
  • Middle layers tend to encode syntax, and deeper layers encode meaning.
  • BertViz makes these patterns visible.

✘ Limits for Khmer

  • Khmer has no spaces between words, so subword tokens split words and attention has to be merged back.
  • Many heads send most of their attention to [CLS] / [SEP] / punctuation.
  • Patterns differ between heads and layers.
  • Shows correlation, not causation.

How to make it scientific

  • Compare with a Khmer dependency treebank.
  • Probing classifiers on the hidden states.
  • Head ablation: switch a head off and see whether the syntax task breaks.
  • Test on many sentences, not just one example.

Example

In «ស្ដេចទៅបាញ់សត្វ», a head where បាញ់ (shoot) attends strongly to ស្ដេច (king) suggests a subject–verb link. But if ស្ដេច is split into subword tokens by XLM-R, or another head shows the opposite pattern, the conclusion becomes weak.

៤
តើ Attention Weight តែមួយមុខគ្រប់គ្រាន់ក្នុងការបកស្រាយហេតុផលរបស់ Model ទាំងស្រុងដែរឬទេ?
Are attention weights alone enough to fully explain the model's reasoning?
VERDICT: NO. ATTENTION SHOWS WHERE THE MODEL LOOKED, NOT WHY IT DECIDED

Why not

  • Residual connections & feed-forward layers carry a lot of information that attention maps don't show.
  • 12 layers × 12 heads = 144 maps in BERT-base, all mixed together. One map is a tiny piece of the picture.
  • Very different attention distributions can give the same prediction (Jain & Wallace, 2019, "Attention is not Explanation").
  • Weights ignore the size of the value vectors, so high attention doesn't always mean high impact.

Better explanation tools

MethodIdea
Attention rollout / flowCombine attention across all layers
Integrated GradientsGradient-based importance of each token
SHAP / LIMERemove words and measure the change
Probing & ablationTest what is encoded and what is causal

Real-world analogy

Eye-tracking a student shows they looked at the right line of the exam, but it doesn't prove they understood it. Attention is the model's "eye-tracking".

SUMMARY

Key takeaways for the C5 discussion

① √dₖ = variance controlIt keeps softmax out of saturation, so the gradients stay healthy (≈0.22 instead of ≈0.0003) and training stays stable.
② Positional encoding = word orderWithout it, a Transformer is a bag of words: «ស្ដេចទៅបាញ់សត្វ» = «សត្វទៅបាញ់ស្ដេច». This is critical for order-based languages like Khmer.
③ Attention ≈ a clue about Khmer syntaxIt can support a claim about syntax. Tokenization, special tokens and head variation limit it, so validate with treebanks, probing and ablation.
④ Attention ≠ full explanationCorrelation, not causation. Combine it with rollout, gradients, SHAP and ablation to explain the model's reasoning.

C5 mindset: don't just describe the attention map. Evaluate what it proves, what it doesn't, and what extra evidence you need.
